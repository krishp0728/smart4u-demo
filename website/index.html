Home Security Systems in Brampton & Across Ontario

Smart4U Security is a home security company based in Brampton, Ontario. We design, install and monitor alarm systems and security cameras for homeowners across the GTA, from Niagara Falls to Kingston and north to Muskoka. Every system starts with a free in-home assessment, because no two homes are the same.

Most alarm systems only go off once a door or window has already been opened. Smart4U's live deterrent cameras are different. You set a zone around your driveway, yard or back door. The moment someone steps into it, the camera sounds a siren and flashes its lights while they're still outside, and you and the monitoring station are alerted right away.

Free installation, one low monthly price

Right now, Smart4U installs your security system for free with a 36-month monitoring plan at $39.95 a month. There's nothing to pay upfront. Our technicians install everything, test it with you, and show you how to use the app before they leave.

No landline? No problem

Our systems connect over a cellular signal, so they keep working without a home phone line. You can arm, disarm and watch your cameras live from anywhere using your smartphone.
