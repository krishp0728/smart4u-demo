Common questions

Is the installation really free?

Yes. Installation costs nothing when you choose our 3-year monitoring plan at $39.95 a month. Over 3 years, that's $1,438.20 plus tax. There's no separate installation or setup bill.

Do I need a home phone line?

No. Our systems connect over a cellular signal, so they keep working without a landline.

I already have an alarm with another company. Can I switch?

Usually, yes. Tell us who your provider is and when your contract ends. We'll look at your current system and tell you honestly when switching makes sense.

What happens if the alarm goes off?

The monitoring station is alerted right away, 24 hours a day, and you get a notification on your phone. With the live deterrent cameras, the siren can go off before anyone reaches your door. Confirm monitoring station name for this answer

Is it hard to use?

No. Our technician shows you everything on install day, and you can arm or disarm from the keypad or your phone. If you get stuck, call us at 905-488-4393.

What happens after 3 years?

We'll contact you before your plan ends to go over your options. Confirm with Smart4U: month-to-month rate, equipment ownership, upgrade offer
