Home Security & Alarm Systems in Brampton, the GTA & Ontario

Smart4U Security is a home security company based in Brampton, Ontario. We design, install and monitor alarm systems and security cameras for homeowners and businesses from Niagara Falls to Kingston and north to Muskoka. With more than 4,000 active customers and authorized Chubb Edwards dealer status, Smart4U gives you professional-grade protection from a company that's local to you.

Every Smart4U system starts with a free in-home assessment. We walk through your home, look at your doors, windows and outdoor areas, and design a system that fits the way you live. Our technicians install everything, test it with you, and make sure you know exactly how it works before they leave.

Smart4U uses cellular connections to monitor your security system, so there's no need for a home phone line. You can arm and disarm your system, watch your cameras live, and receive instant alerts on your smartphone from anywhere.

Live Deterrent Security: Stop Intruders Before They Get In

Most alarm systems only go off once a door or window has already been opened. Smart4U's live deterrent cameras are different. You choose a zone around your driveway, yard or back door. The moment someone steps into it, the camera sounds a siren and flashes its lights while they're still outside, and you and the monitoring station are alerted right away.
